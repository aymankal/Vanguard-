Thesis

B2B AI, Fintech and SaaS first. Tech-enabled services and cybersecurity when the fit is strong. Revenue or real pilots beat slides. Founders who know the market from the inside beat everyone.

Our edge: operator capital

Portfolio companies get access to what Vanguard runs every day: outbound SDR pods, sales playbooks, CRM and automation setup, and customer support. Capital plus pipeline, not capital plus advice.

How we pick

Every deal gets a Frontier Fit Score out of 100: sector 25, stage 20, traction 20, founder quality 35. Founder quality only counts after a partner approves a structured vetting with references.

Where deals come from

Founder referrals first, then Boardy, VC Lab, LvlUp, Decile, LinkedIn and inbound. Scouts source and vet. Partners decide.

The path to a check

  1. SourcedScout submits the startup and the case for it.
  2. QualifiedPartner approves it into the pipeline.
  3. First callFounder meeting, notes logged on the deal.
  4. DiligenceFounder vetting, references, traction checked against documents.
  5. IC reviewPartners review the file and the Fit Score.
  6. Term sheetCheck size, instrument, terms.
  7. InvestedGTM support from Vanguard starts.
