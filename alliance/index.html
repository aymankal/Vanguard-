What it is

Pre-seed capital with a sales team attached.

Frontier Alliance is the community and event series behind Vanguard Frontier Fund I, a $1.5M pre-seed and seed fund backing B2B AI, fintech and SaaS companies.

Every event is small and curated. Founders show real traction, investors see deal flow early, and operators share what works. Our portfolio companies get more than a cheque: Vanguard's SDR and sales teams help them build pipeline from day one.

Who it's for

Three seats at the table.

Founders

Pre-seed and seed B2B teams in AI, fintech or SaaS. Meet investors who back early, get candid feedback, and see how a sales team attached to your round changes your first year.

Investors

Angels, funds and syndicates looking for early B2B deal flow from Lebanon, the region and the diaspora. Co-invest alongside Fund I.

Operators

Sales, growth and product leaders who have scaled B2B companies. Mentor founders, join panels, and get first look at what is being built.

    FAQ

    Questions

    How do I register?

    Click Register on any event. Luma's registration window opens right here. If it doesn't, you'll go to the event page on Luma. Your confirmation and calendar invite come from Luma.

    Does it cost anything?

    The ticket type and any price are shown on each event's Luma page before you confirm.

    Is every registration accepted?

    Some events are curated and need host approval to keep the room balanced between founders, investors and operators. Luma emails you as soon as you're approved.

    Can I pitch at an event?

    Founder slots are picked from registrants. Register as a founder, tell us what you're building in the Luma form, and we'll reach out if there's a fit.

    How do I hear about new events?

    Subscribe to the Frontier Alliance calendar on Luma. New events land in your inbox and calendar automatically.
